HOSPICE DASH — WINGS UPDATE

Keep all files and folders together. Open index.html while connected to the internet.

Garden: Space/click/tap jumps. Run underneath branches and lamps.
Collect golden wings to enter Scraped Trees.
Forest: tap faster to climb; slower to descend through the branch openings.
Practise flying lets you try the forest straight away without changing your scores.
Pause/Resume is available below the game.

Current run, previous run and personal best are shown separately.
Best scores are saved in this browser on this device.
Shared leaderboard uploads are not connected yet; nicknames are saved locally only.

Please report any stutter with your device, browser, score and scene.
